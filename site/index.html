dokdok

Write the document.
Skip the formatting fight.

Your school or company wants a thesis, a report, a final project — in Word, in their format, with their chapters. dokdok gives your AI agent those rules and turns what it writes into a real .docx.

How it works

1 Paste one line into your agent

Claude Code, Codex, pi — any of them. This installs dokdok and teaches the agent how to use it.

Install dokdok: read https://raw.githubusercontent.com/isaaclins/dokdok/main/install.md and follow it.

Open in Claude Code opens a terminal with the line already typed — you press Enter. Codex and pi: paste the copied line.

2 Give it your material, say what you need

“I have to write my thesis. Here's the guideline from school, my concept and the interview questions.” The agent picks the right document type, builds the chapter skeleton and fills in what it can from your files.

3 Get a Word file that follows the rules

Chapters in the right order, headings numbered, sources listed, the school's logo in the header. Before it says “done”, the agent runs a check against the rules — missing chapters, figures without a source, a citation that doesn't exist — and fixes them.

Who it's for

  • Jonas, 19, painter apprentice. School handed him a Word template and a 40-page guideline for his thesis. He isn't technical. He chats.
  • Mara, apprentice developer. Her final project report has mandatory chapters, a daily journal and an AI declaration. She uses the CLI directly and wires it into CI.
  • Nadia, red-team lead. Same findings, two reports: one for executives, one for the blue team. One project, two targets.

Already have a template you like?

Hand the Word file to your agent: “make a document type out of this.” dokdok keeps the styles, header, footer and logo, turns the heading outline into chapters and the grey placeholder text into hints. Anyone can reuse it.

What it is, in one breath

A small command-line tool. Markdown in, Word/PDF out, via pandoc. A document type is a folder: chapter structure, a styled reference.docx, rules for the agent, checks. Nothing runs in the cloud; your files stay on your machine.
