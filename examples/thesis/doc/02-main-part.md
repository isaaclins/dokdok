## How a colony survives winter

Honey bees do not hibernate. The colony forms a tight cluster around the queen and keeps its core at about 20 °C by vibrating flight muscles, burning stored honey as fuel [@survey2025]. City colonies benefit from slightly warmer surroundings, but they also face longer flying seasons and more varied forage.

## Autumn preparations

The most important decisions fall in late summer: treating against the varroa mite, leaving enough honey stores, and reducing the hive entrance against mice. Colonies with a high mite load in August rarely see spring [@beeguide2024].
