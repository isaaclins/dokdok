## Topic and personal motivation

My uncle keeps two hives on a garage roof in the middle of town. Two winters ago one colony did not make it, and nobody could say why. I want to understand what a city colony needs to survive the cold months and what a beekeeper can do about it.

## Aim and research questions

The aim of this thesis is to explain how honey bee colonies in cities overwinter and which beekeeping practices improve their chances.

1. How does a colony survive winter, and what makes city colonies different?
2. Which preparations in autumn matter most?
3. How can a beekeeper tell in spring whether the colony is healthy?

## Approach and methods

I reviewed beekeeping literature and a national colony-loss survey [@survey2025], interviewed an urban beekeeper, and kept a diary of my uncle's two hives from October to March.
