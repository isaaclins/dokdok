# Bachelor Thesis Guideline
## Hochschule Rheintal — School of Digital Business

**Version:** 4.2 · Valid from Autumn Semester 2024
**Applies to:** BSc Digital Business bachelor theses

---

## 1. Purpose

The bachelor thesis demonstrates that the student can independently address a practical or research-oriented problem within Digital Business using appropriate academic methods, within a defined time frame.

## 2. Required Structure

The thesis must contain the following sections, in this order:

1. **Title page** — thesis title, author name and matriculation number, degree program, supervisor and second examiner, institution, submission date.
2. **Abstract** — 150–250 words, English, with 4–6 keywords.
3. **Table of contents**
4. **Introduction** — motivation, problem statement, research questions, and a brief outline of the methodology.
5. **Literature review** — theoretical background and identification of the research gap.
6. **Methods** — research design, sampling, data collection, and analysis approach.
7. **Results** — presentation of findings; at least one table and one figure are required.
8. **Discussion** — interpretation of results, practical implications, and limitations.
9. **Conclusion** — summary of findings and outlook for future work.
10. **References** — APA 7 format (see separate citation handout).
11. **Appendix** — survey instruments, interview guides, or supplementary data.
12. **Declaration of authorship** — mandatory (see Section 6).
13. **AI-use declaration** — mandatory (see Section 7).

## 3. Length and Format

- **Word count:** 8,000–10,000 words (excluding title page, references, and appendix).
- **Font:** serif typeface (e.g., Georgia or Times New Roman), 11–12 pt body.
- **Line spacing:** 1.5.
- **Margins:** 2.5 cm on all sides.
- **Page numbers:** bottom, starting from the introduction.
- **Figures and tables:** numbered consecutively, each with a caption; refer to every figure/table in the text.
- **Language:** English or German (must be consistent throughout).

## 4. Citations

All sources must be cited in **APA 7th edition** style, both in-text and in the reference list. See the accompanying handout *Zitierregeln_APA.md*. Uncited use of third-party material is treated as plagiarism.

## 5. Submission and Deadline

- **Deadline:** 12 June 2025, 23:59 CET, via the learning platform.
- One PDF file, named `Thesis_<Lastname>_<Topic>.pdf`.
- Late submissions are downgraded by one grade per started 24 hours, unless a documented extension was granted.

## 6. Declaration of Authorship (mandatory)

The thesis must include a signed statement confirming that the work was produced independently, that all sources are cited, and that the thesis has not been submitted elsewhere. Suggested wording:

> "I hereby declare that I have written this thesis independently, that I have used no sources or aids other than those indicated, and that all passages taken verbatim or in substance from published or unpublished works have been marked as such."

## 7. AI-Use Declaration (mandatory)

Students must disclose any use of generative AI tools (e.g., text generation, translation, code assistance). The declaration must state which tools were used, for which purpose, and in which sections. Undeclared substantial AI use is a disciplinary matter. Suggested wording:

> "I declare that generative AI tools were used as follows: [tool, purpose, sections]. All AI-assisted content was reviewed and verified by the author, who takes full responsibility for the final text."

## 8. Grading Rubric (100 points)

| Criterion                              | Points |
|----------------------------------------|--------|
| Problem framing & research questions   | 10     |
| Literature review & theoretical basis  | 15     |
| Methodology (design & rigor)           | 20     |
| Results & analysis                     | 20     |
| Discussion & critical reflection       | 15     |
| Structure, clarity & academic writing  | 10     |
| Citation accuracy (APA 7)              | 5      |
| Formal compliance & declarations       | 5      |
| **Total**                              | **100**|

Grades follow the standard 1–6 scale; a minimum of 60 points (grade 4.0) is required to pass. Missing or false declarations (Sections 6–7) can result in a failing grade regardless of content.

## 9. Supervision

Each student is assigned a supervisor. At least two consultation meetings are expected: one after the proposal is approved and one before the results chapter is finalized.
