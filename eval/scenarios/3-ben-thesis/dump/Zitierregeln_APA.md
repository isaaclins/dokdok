
# APA 7 Quick Reference
## Hochschule Rheintal — School of Digital Business

Use this one-page handout for all in-text citations and reference lists. When in doubt, consult the official *Publication Manual of the American Psychological Association* (7th ed.).

---

## In-Text Citations

Cite the author's surname and year of publication.

- **Paraphrase:** `(Venkatesh et al., 2012)` or narratively: `Venkatesh et al. (2012) argue that ...`
- **One author:** `(Gössling, 2020)`
- **Two authors:** `(Fitt & Curl, 2020)` — use "&" inside parentheses, "and" in running text.
- **Three or more authors:** `(Sanders et al., 2020)` — always use "et al." from the first citation.
- **Multiple sources in one parenthesis:** separate with semicolons, alphabetically: `(Fitt & Curl, 2020; Gössling, 2020)`.
- **No date:** use `(n.d.)`.

## Direct Quotes

Include the page number.

- Short quote (< 40 words): in quotation marks in the text — `"user acceptance is context-dependent" (Venkatesh et al., 2003, p. 431)`.
- Long quote (≥ 40 words): block quote, indented, no quotation marks, citation after the final punctuation.

## Reference List

Alphabetical by first author's surname; hanging indent; double-spaced. Include a DOI or URL where available.

### Book
> Author, A. A. (Year). *Title of work: Capital letter for subtitle* (edition). Publisher.

Example:
> Rogers, E. M. (2003). *Diffusion of innovations* (5th ed.). Free Press.

### Journal Article
> Author, A. A., & Author, B. B. (Year). Title of the article. *Journal Name, Volume*(Issue), pages. https://doi.org/xxxx

Example:
> Gössling, S. (2020). Integrating e-scooters in urban transportation. *Transportation Research Part D, 79*, 102230. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.trd.2020.102230

### Website
> Author or Organization. (Year, Month Day). *Title of page*. Site Name. URL

Example:
> Bundesamt für Statistik. (2023, March 14). *Detailhandel in der Schweiz*. BFS. https://www.bfs.admin.ch

---

**Common mistakes to avoid**

- Do not list sources that are not cited in the text (and vice versa).
- Capitalize only the first word of article titles and subtitles (sentence case).
- Journal names and volume numbers are italicized; issue numbers are not.
- Always add the retrieval URL/DOI for online sources.
